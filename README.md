## What I build

### Natural-language data analytics

I develop systems that translate business questions into SQL and return results as tables, charts, and concise explanations. My work includes schema understanding, multi-database adapters, safe query execution, response streaming, evaluation feedback, and evidence tracing.

### RAG, GraphRAG, and enterprise knowledge

I design retrieval systems that combine vector search, business terminology, metadata, and knowledge graphs. I also build evaluation workflows to distinguish retrieval quality from final-answer quality and make the evidence behind AI responses visible to users.

### Privacy-aware synthetic data

I work on tabular synthetic-data workflows covering generation, privacy-risk assessment, utility evaluation, automatic optimization, and review-ready reporting. A recurring goal is to preserve analytical value while reducing disclosure risk.

### Production-ready AI products

I enjoy taking prototypes beyond the demo stage: authentication, role-based access, audit logs, observability, offline distribution, containerized deployment, and automated delivery are all part of the product—not afterthoughts.
